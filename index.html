AIDGO — AI Data Governance Ontology

ISO/IEC 5259 ↔ EU AI Act Article 10 · Legal-Technical Alignment

AIDGO is an OWL 2 ontology for expressing alignment between ISO/IEC 5259 data quality management requirements and EU AI Act Article 10 data governance obligations. It introduces five alignment relation types, uses RDF-star annotated triples for full provenance, and is fully queryable by SPARQL.

AIDGO is designed to be extensible: the same alignment schema and predicate vocabulary applies to any legal instrument paired with any technical standard. It is developed at the ADAPT Centre as part of the AIDGO project on AI Data Governance.

Abstract

AIDGO bridges the normative requirements of the EU AI Act Article 10 with the technical framework of ISO/IEC 5259 by representing their semantic alignment as machine-readable, SPARQL-querable RDF assertions.

The ontology introduces five alignment relation classes that form an exhaustive taxonomy of possible legal-technical relationships: CompletelySatisfies, PartiallySatisfies, RelatedTo, ConflictsWith, and NoAlignment. Every mapping triple is annotated with provenance metadata using PROV-O and carries an alignment justification, enabling reproducible conformance assessment.

AIDGO adopts the TAIR (Technical-Legal Alignment in Requirements) framework and the OSLC Requirements Management (OSLC-RM) vocabulary. Requirements are decomposed to the smallest independently verifiable atomic unit — tair:ConjuntiveRequirement — using the bracket-context pattern, ensuring that every node in the knowledge graph represents a distinct, independently queryable obligation.

An interactive knowledge graph explorer is available at aidgo_knowledge_graph.html, providing force-directed visualisation of all 332 nodes and their relationships.
